# Valnivo feedback

This repository is for **bug reports and ideas** about
[Valnivo](https://ledgger.web.app) — see where your money could take you.

There is no code here. It exists so anyone can report a problem or suggest a
feature without an account on anything but GitHub.

- [Report a bug](https://github.com/kabilJbeli/valnivo-feedback/issues/new?template=bug.yml)
- [Suggest a feature](https://github.com/kabilJbeli/valnivo-feedback/issues/new?template=feature.yml)
- [See what has already been reported](https://github.com/kabilJbeli/valnivo-feedback/issues)

## Please do not post private information

Issues here are public. Never paste your figures, a backup file, screenshots of
your ledger, your recovery code, or anything from your Google account. A
description of what went wrong is enough; if something can only be explained
with your data, email <support@valnivo.eu> instead.

## Security problems

Please do not open a public issue. Email <support@valnivo.eu> and give it
a few days before sharing it anywhere.
